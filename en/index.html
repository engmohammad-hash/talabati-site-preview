    FAQ

    Everything you need to know

    What is Talabati?

    Talabati is a delivery app that gathers your stores in one place: restaurants, groceries, pharmacies and more. You pick a store, fill your cart, and a driver brings the order to your door where you pay in cash.

    How do I download the app?

    Tap the Download button at the top of this page and the installer file downloads to your Android phone. Open the file when it finishes and allow installation from an outside source — a normal Android prompt for any app not installed from the store — and you are ready. Step-by-step pictures for Xiaomi, Samsung and iPhone are in the install guide.

    My phone says it can't install unknown apps. What do I do?

    It is Android's usual safety message for any app installed from outside the store. Tap “Settings” in that message, turn on “Allow from this source”, go back and tap “Install”. You only do this once.

    How do I update the app?

    When a new version is out, the app shows “A new update is available”. Tap “Update now”, the new version downloads from our site, and you install it the same way. Your account and orders stay as they are.

    Does Talabati work on iPhone?

    Yes. The iPhone version runs in Safari: tap “Download for iPhone”, then the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. The Talabati icon appears among your apps and opens full screen. The same account works on Android and iPhone.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Downloading and using the app is completely free — no monthly subscription and no sign-up fee. You only pay for your order and the delivery fee, and you see both before you confirm.

    How do I pay?

    Cash on delivery only. You pay the driver when the order reaches you. There are no bank cards, no e-wallets and no prepayment.

    What currency are prices in?

    Every price in the app is in the new Syrian pound, and that is what you pay. Some stores price their products in US dollars; the app converts at the exchange rate the store sets, and you always see pounds.

    What is the pickup code?

    The pickup code is a number tied to your order and shown in the app. When the driver arrives you give them the code, and the order is not marked delivered until they enter it. Nobody else can take your order, and the driver has proof of delivery.

    How is the delivery fee calculated?

    The fee is based on the real road distance between the store and your address — not a straight line and not an estimate. You see the exact fee before you confirm the order.

    Can I order from more than one store at once?

    Yes. The Talabati cart accepts products from several stores at the same time, and each store prepares its own part of the order. You see each store separately inside your cart.

    How do I add my store to Talabati?

    Tap “Join as a store” in the stores section and message us on WhatsApp. We open a store account for you to manage your sections, products, prices — in pounds or dollars — orders, deals and daily sales.
